{{ _("What the site advised, as it stood when each match started.") }} {% trans %}Each tip is saved every time Betano's lines are collected and priced, and locked the moment its game starts — the database refuses any later change. Nothing here is recalculated; only the score is looked up. Tips are over/under lines on points - both teams' total, or one team's - for the whole game (overtime included, as Betano settles them) or the first half, at +6% value or more on the model's chance blended 3:1 with Betano's own (margin taken out), at most one per game and half.{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}How a line is priced. The expected points are DraftKings' line for the game (its total, split between the teams by its spread; a first half by the share of the points first halves score). Around it, a spread measured on three seasons of results against the closing line: in the NBA the total lands within 18 points of the close two times in three. A team-rating model is kept alongside but barely moves the centre - on past NBA seasons it added nothing to the closing line.{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}Stake is in tenths of a unit: 10/10 is a full unit, whatever size you choose for one. It is quarter Kelly on the model's chance blended half and half with the bookmaker's, lower for long odds, and tips on the same game share one stake.{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}Results and lines: ESPN; EuroLeague and EuroCup from the EuroLeague's own data.{% endtrans %}
