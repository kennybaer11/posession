The rule: bet when the model has beaten the market by more than luck explains — not when it claims an edge. A model's own edge estimate is a claim about itself; the only thing that can check it is a settled record. Two standard errors is the usual bar, and at these prices that means roughly 100–200 settled bets. That is not pessimism, it is what it costs to tell a real 5% edge from a coin that landed well.

You do not have to stake money to get there. Every recorded line is graded whether or not a bet was placed, so the record builds itself for free.
